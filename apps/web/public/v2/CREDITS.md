# /v2 assets

Everything in this folder (illustrations, hero video, decor, icons, logos) is taken from the
cofounder.co homepage (The General Intelligence Company of New York; art by Altalogy) on
1 Nov 2026. The project owner stated they have permission to use these files.

Keep `/v2` out of search (the route sets `noindex`), and replace these files with Krew's own
before anything here ships publicly.

Fonts: Neoris (licensed to Cofounder) is **not** included; `/v2` uses Lexend Deca in its place.
Departure Mono (`app/v2/fonts`) is SIL Open Font License. IBM Plex Mono and Inter load from
Google Fonts via `next/font`.
